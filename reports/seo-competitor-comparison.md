## Four priorities for Ankita

1. **Create one genuinely useful bridal service landing page, linked from homepage/services/gallery.** The current [services](https://www.theglambyankita.com/services) is a helpful multi-service overview but does not offer the same focused bridal enquiry destination as [Elise's bridal page](https://www.elisebysouth.com/bridal) or [Alana's bridal page](https://www.alanamuha.com/bridal-makeup-and-hair-artistry). Give it a unique natural title and first-hand details: what's included in Ankita's $180 bridal makeup, whether trials are available (and at what cost), time required, party scheduling, touch-ups, venue/mobile constraints, and exactly how a bride should enquire. Clarify terms rather than borrowing competitors' packages or adding geographic doorway variants.
2. **Curate real bridal proof within the existing gallery.** Keep the current filters, captions and TFP attribution, but group actual completed bridal looks with occasion/context, finish and what Ankita actually did; link the bridal page to those examples. [Alana's bridal portfolio](https://www.alanamuha.com/bridal-portfolio) identifies glam levels and [Elise's Real Brides portfolio](https://www.elisebysouth.com/portfolio) frames work around the client occasion. Distinguish a real wedding from a styled/TFP shoot, and remove or clearly label any public “Coming Soon” cards rather than filling them with fabricated work.
3. **Make existing trust evidence more decision-specific.** Ankita already has five named testimonials and a review link on the [homepage](https://www.theglambyankita.com/); do not claim reviews are absent. With client permission, add verified bridal/event context beside relevant looks or service sections and link to the original public review when available. [Natasha's client accounts](https://www.bynatasha.com.au/) identify the occasion and client locality; [Elise's homepage](https://www.elisebysouth.com/) includes a bride's account of the preview-to-wedding process. Do not invent wedding clients, ratings, review counts or venues.
4. **Explain the booking journey and any variable costs beside the relevant CTA.** The current [contact form](https://www.theglambyankita.com/contact) already collects date, group size, service and studio/mobile choice, but can state what happens **after** submission: when availability and final price are confirmed, whether a deposit/trial is required, and how travel or early starts are quoted if applicable. [JPC's bridal page](https://www.melbournemakeupartist.com/near-me-makeup-artist) and [Elise's homepage](https://www.elisebysouth.com/) make the enquiry-to-confirmation steps explicit. Retain the truthful Southbank studio and conditional mobile availability; don't copy JPC's all-Melbourne mobile promise.
